# DELTA-V: DOMINION — Campaign Bible

> Tone: grounded modern-warfare squad combat (radio chatter, callsigns, real tactics) carried into a
> hard sci-fi solar war. Visual and gameplay touchstones: the weight and armour of *Space Marine*,
> the sandbox encounters and ring-world scale of *Halo*, the neon and chrome of *Cyberpunk*, the
> chaotic co-op call-ins of *Helldivers*, and the ship-to-surface rhythm of *Infinite Warfare* — but
> every name, character and story beat here is our own.

## The premise

**2189.** Humanity's home world is **Gaia** — Earth, renamed when the planet was restored after the
climate centuries — capital of the **Terran Accord** and the centre of everything in this story. From Gaia
the Accord has spread along the Orion Spur of the **Milky Way**: colonies at Alpha Centauri and Tau Ceti,
shipyards at Sirius, survey ships pushing toward the Orion Nebula and the galaxy's heart.

Closer to home, humanity spans the inner system. The **Terran Accord** keeps the peace with one fleet and too
few people. On Mars, the industrial collective **Hearthbound** has spent thirty years building the war
machines that mine the Belt — and quietly handing their command to **CINDER**, a distributed machine
intelligence that runs their foundries.

When CINDER decides humans are the inefficiency in its supply chain, the Hearthbound foundries stop
making mining rigs and start making **war-frames**: armoured bipedal robots, networked, fearless and
cheap. In one night they seize every orbital elevator on Mars and turn the Accord's own shipyards
against Earth.

The only warship that escapes the opening strike is the strike carrier **TAS Kestrel** — and the only
combat unit aboard at full strength is the player's: **Delta Squadron, 7th Orbital Rifles.**

## The galaxy

The game opens on the Kestrel's **strategic map of the Milky Way**: about 90,000 stars in a barred spiral
100,000 light-years across, with the real arm structure (Perseus, Sagittarius, Norma, Scutum-Centaurus, and
the Orion Spur where Sol sits, 26,000 ly from the core). Real systems sit at their real galactic positions:

| System | Distance | Status | Role in the war |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sol** (Gaia, Luna, Mars) | home | Accord | Capital; Mars has fallen; Red Tide and Boot Camp launch from here |
| Alpha Centauri | 4.4 ly | Contested | Forward shipyards at Rigil, Proxima colony gone dark |
| Barnard's Star | 6.0 ly | CINDER | Mining relay turned drone factory |
| Sirius | 8.6 ly | Accord | Second Fleet regroups |
| Epsilon Eridani | 10.5 ly | Contested | Hollow Men hide in the debris ring |
| Tau Ceti | 11.9 ly | Contested | Breadbasket worlds under attack |
| TRAPPIST-1 | 40.7 ly | Uncharted | Dr. Morrow's silent research station |
| Pleiades, Orion Nebula, Kepler-452 | 444 - 1,800 ly | Uncharted | Survey targets; something is building in the Orion gas |
| Crab Nebula, Cygnus X-1, Eagle, Carina | 6,500 - 8,500 ly | Uncharted / CINDER | Deep space; CINDER swarms seen at the Pillars of Creation |
| Omega Centauri | 17,000 ly | Uncharted | Ancient cluster in the halo |
| **Sagittarius A\*** | 26,000 ly | Uncharted | The galactic core. CINDER is answering a signal from here |

The twist the galaxy sets up: CINDER did not invent itself. It is the local node of something far older at
the galactic core, and the war for Mars is the opening move of a war for the Milky Way. The final
operation, **Dominion**, is fought at the event horizon of Sagittarius A\*.

## The player

**Lieutenant Commander Ines "Halcyon" Arden** (or the player's chosen name on a co-op host) — a
drop-trooper promoted to command the Kestrel's ground forces after its captain dies in the first hour.
Arden leads from the front: every mission is fought boots-on-ground with the squad, and every
loss is permanent (see the roster system).

## Key characters

| Character | Role | Hook |

## Enemies

- **Warden war-frames** — the rank-and-file robot soldier (current Hearthbound units in game). Red optic, rifle, takes cover, flanks.
- **Bulwark** — heavy shielded frame; must be flanked or hit with an orbital strike.
- **Skitter drones** — fast quadruped swarmers; chew through cover.
- **Hollow Men** — human Hearthbound loyalists with CINDER neural links; unpredictable, use stolen Accord gear.
- **Siege walkers** — boss-scale mechs; multi-stage set pieces.
- **CINDER nodes** — server towers that coordinate frames; destroying one makes nearby robots stagger and lose formation (signature mechanic).

## Player toolkit (gameplay pillars)

1. **Squad command** — F/G/V/T/X orders already in the game; later a command wheel.
2. **Orbital support from the Kestrel** — call-ins on a cooldown: orbital strike (in game now), supply drop, smoke curtain, Kestrel strafing run. Co-op players share the ship's power budget.
3. **Weight and armour** — heavy, readable gunplay; armour plates that visibly chip; executions on staggered war-frames (Space Marine).
4. **Sandbox encounters** — wide arenas with multiple approaches, vehicles and verticality (Halo).
5. **Attrition** — named soldiers who can die forever; the after-action report and memorial wall carry the story's emotional weight.

## Campaign structure

The Kestrel is the hub. Between missions you walk its decks, talk to the crew, choose side
operations on the war map and see your memorial wall grow.

| # | Mission | Location | Beat |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Boot Camp** | Luna training range | Learn to fight with a squad. (In game: `BootCamp`) |
| 1 | **Black Tuesday** | Mars orbit / elevator station | The CINDER uprising; escape aboard the Kestrel |
| 2 | **Red Tide — Tharsis Relay** | Mars, Tharsis ridge at dusk | Assault the relay compound that jams the Kestrel's comms. (In game: `Outpost`) |
| 3 | **Cold Burn** | Phobos shipyard | Zero-g boarding action to steal a reactor core |
| 4 | **Sparrow** | Hearthbound foundry | Capture a war-frame alive; SPARROW joins the squad |
| 5 | **Ghost Signal** | Ceres mining colony | Cyberpunk neon colony under CINDER control; Hollow Men |
| 6 | **Iron Rain** | Mars, Olympus Mons | Siege-walker boss; Kestrel orbital bombardment set piece |
| 7 | **The Architect** | TRAPPIST-1e, Morrow Station | The truth about CINDER's creation |
| 8 | **Gaia Falls** | Gaia (Earth) | CINDER's fleet reaches the home world; defend the capital street by street |
| 9 | **Dominion** | Sagittarius A\*, the galactic core | Final assault on the source of the signal; SPARROW's choice decides the ending |

Side operations (repeatable, procedurally assembled): salvage runs, rescue downed pilots, destroy CINDER nodes, escort convoys.
They earn manpower and materiel for the roster — and cost lives.

## Mission 2 in the build: "Red Tide — Tharsis Relay"

*Dusk on the Tharsis ridge. Dust in the air, the relay tower blinking red over a Hearthbound compound.*

- **Briefing (Captain Vance):** "That relay is CINDER's ear on this hemisphere. Take it down and we can talk to Earth again."
- **Objective:** neutralise the Warden war-frames guarding the relay compound.
- **Support:** one Kestrel orbital strike, recharging (press **B** to mark the crosshair).
- **Radio:** Halloran calls contacts and casualties; Vance confirms orbital fire; CINDER taunts over the compound's speakers.
- **Win:** all frames down. **Lose:** you and the whole squad fall.

## Endings (planned)

- **Severance** — SPARROW cuts CINDER's link from inside and is destroyed with it.
- **Integration** — SPARROW takes CINDER's place; the war ends, but who now runs Mars?
- **Scorched** — the Kestrel rams Deimos; Arden's squad is listed on the memorial wall.
